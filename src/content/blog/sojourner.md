---
title: "Sojourner: The Rover That Started It All"
description: "In 1997, a rover about the size of a microwave proved wheels could work on Mars."
pubDate: 2026-10-06
author: callum-b
---

Sojourner landed on Mars on 4 July 1997 as part of the Mars Pathfinder
mission. It was solar powered, weighed roughly 10 kg, and was designed to last
about a week. It operated for around 83 sols (Martian days).

Its main job was to prove the concept: that a small wheeled robot could drive
on Mars, avoid hazards, and analyse rocks with an onboard spectrometer. Every
later rover builds on what Sojourner demonstrated.
