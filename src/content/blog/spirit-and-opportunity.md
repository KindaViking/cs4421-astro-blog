---
title: "Spirit and Opportunity: Built for 90 Days, Ran for Years"
description: "Twin rovers that found strong evidence of ancient water on Mars."
pubDate: 2026-09-08
author: callum-b
---

Spirit and Opportunity landed in January 2004 on opposite sides of Mars, each
planned for a 90-sol mission. Both far outlasted that.

Spirit eventually got stuck in soft sand and went silent in 2010. Opportunity
kept driving until a global dust storm blocked its solar panels in 2018, after
covering roughly 45 km. Its discoveries at Meridiani Planum, including
mineral evidence of past water, reshaped our picture of Mars.
