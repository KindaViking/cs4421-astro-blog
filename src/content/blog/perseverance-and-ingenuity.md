pubDate: 2026-09-22
author: callum-b
---

Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater in February 2021, an ancient river delta
chosen because it may preserve signs of past life. The rover collects rock
samples in sealed tubes, and carried an experiment called MOXIE that made
oxygen from Mars's carbon dioxide atmosphere.

It also brought along Ingenuity, a small helicopter. In April 2021 Ingenuity
made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet. It went on to fly
72 times before its mission ended in January 2024.
