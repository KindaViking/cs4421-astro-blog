title: "Curiosity: A Car-Sized Lab on Wheels"
description: "How a nuclear-powered rover and a sky crane changed Mars exploration."
pubDate: 2026-09-15
author: callum-b
---

Curiosity landed in Gale Crater in August 2012 using the "sky crane", a
rocket-powered platform that lowered the rover on cables. It is roughly the
size of a small car and carries a suite of instruments to analyse rock and soil.

Rather than solar panels, it uses a radioisotope power generator, which turns
the heat from decaying plutonium into electricity. That keeps it running
through dust and winter. Curiosity showed Gale Crater once had conditions
suitable for microbial life.
